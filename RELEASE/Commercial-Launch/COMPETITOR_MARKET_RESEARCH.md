## 1. Executive Summary

This study delivers an in-depth competitive market analysis of medical clinic management software across **Morocco** and **six key European healthcare markets** (France, Belgium, Spain, Portugal, Germany, and the Netherlands).

Every competitor profile was researched using live web searches, official vendor portals, industry pricing databases, and professional medical user feedback gathered on November 11, 2026.

### Key Macro Market Findings:
1. **The Recurring SaaS Lock-in Reality:** Across Western Europe, modern practice management software is almost universally sold as a **recurring monthly/annual SaaS subscription** ranging from **€900 to over €3,000 per doctor every single year** (e.g. Doctolib Pro, Weda, CareConnect, Gesden ONE).
2. **The Legacy On-Premise Reality:** Legacy desktop systems that offer local licenses (e.g. Dampsoft in Germany, Julie Solutions in France, Clinosys in Morocco) carry exorbitant initial purchase fees (€1,000 to €16,000), require rigid per-workstation client licenses, and enforce mandatory annual maintenance contracts of €700 to €1,500/year.
3. **The Multi-Specialty Vacuum:** Most market solutions are strictly siloed: they either serve general medicine *or* dental surgery *or* optometry. Clinics grouping dentists, ophthalmologists, and general physicians are forced to pay for multiple disconnected systems.
4. **MediCore's Disruptive Value Proposition:** A modern, beautiful web-based EMR (Laravel 12 / React 19) uniting **General Medicine + FDI Dental Odontogram + OD/OS Optical Refraction + Smart Waiting Queue + Cashier Billing + Pharmacy Inventory** sold under a **perpetual one-time commercial license with full unencrypted source code**.
