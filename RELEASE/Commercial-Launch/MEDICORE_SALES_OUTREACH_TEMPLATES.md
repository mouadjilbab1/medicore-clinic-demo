## 1. Outreach Philosophy & Execution Guidelines

### 1.1 Core Value Proposition Matrix
When contacting clinic owners, medical directors, and lead practitioners, our communication must never look like mass spam. Every touchpoint is **strictly targeted, role-relevant, respectful of professional time**, and centered on the four primary doctor pain points:
1. **Recurring SaaS Fatigue:** Emphasize zero monthly subscription fees and permanent software ownership.
2. **Data Sovereignty & Medical Secrecy:** Highlight that patient health records stay inside their clinic (local server, private cPanel, or dedicated VPS), with zero third-party commercial data harvesting.
3. **Clinical Precision:** Address dental FDI charting, ophthalmic sphere/cylinder refractions, and real-time waiting room queue triage.
4. **Instant Self-Hosted Demo:** Invite them to test live with pre-populated, realistic medical demo data.
