## 1. Executive Summary & Market Positioning

### 1.1 The Market Disruption Imperative
Healthcare practice management software (PMS) in Western Europe and North Africa is dominated by two polar opposites:
1. **Aggressive Recurring Cloud SaaS:** Systems like Doctolib Pro (€1,668/year), Weda (€1,920/year), and CareConnect (€1,853/year) lock doctors into perpetual subscriptions, extract price increases annually, and hold patient data on proprietary remote clouds.
2. **Obsolete Heavy On-Premise Software:** Systems like Julie Solutions (€1,600+ initial + €800/year support) or Clinosys (4,000–12,000 MAD) require Windows-specific server setups, complex VPN configurations for remote access, and archaic user interfaces.

**MediCore Clinic occupies the high-growth sweet spot:**
- **Modern Web Technology:** High-performance Laravel 11 + React 18 single-page application with responsive RTL/LTR design.
- **100% Data Sovereignty:** Self-hosted on local clinic networks (LAN), private cPanel, or sovereign cloud VPS. Full compliance with **EU GDPR** and **Moroccan CNDP Law 09-08**.
- **Transparent One-Time Ownership:** Zero mandatory recurring fees. Doctors own their system and data permanently.

## 5. Local Payment Methods & Frictionless Checkout

### 5.1 Morocco Payment Infrastructure
1. **Direct Moroccan Bank Transfer (Virement Bancaire):**
   - Free, instantaneous between identical banks (Attijariwafa Bank, CIH Bank, Bank of Africa, BCP).
   - High trust factor for Moroccan physicians who prefer paying verified corporate RIB accounts.
2. **Local Payment Gateway (CMI / PayZone / FastPay):**
   - Allows instant payment via Moroccan domestic credit/debit cards (Carte CMI).
3. **Cash / Agency Transfer (Cash Plus, Wafacash):**
   - Ideal for traditional practitioners without online payment cards. Instant license delivery upon WhatsApp voucher verification.

### 5.2 Europe Payment Infrastructure
1. **Stripe Embedded Checkout:**
   - Visa, Mastercard, American Express.
   - **SEPA Direct Debit** (standard across Eurozone B2B transactions).
   - **iDEAL** (dominant in the Netherlands — 70%+ of online transactions).
   - **Bancontact** (dominant in Belgium).
   - **Giropay / Sofort** (popular in Germany).
   - **Bizum / Redsys** (widely accepted in Spain).
2. **B2B Bank Wire (IBAN/BIC with SEPA Instant):**
   - Proforma invoice generated automatically with EU VAT validation (VIES Reverse Charge for cross-border B2B).
3. **PayPal for Business:**
   - Instant buyer protection and corporate debit cards.

## 6. Commercial Licensing Enforcement & Terms Alignment

In accordance with `LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md`:
1. **Single Installation per License (Solo/Polyclinic):** Each license grants the buyer the legal right to operate the software on one primary production instance (plus one staging/testing instance).
2. **Reseller Rights (Tier 3):** Grants 5 deployment activation keys. The licensee may bill the end client any amount for hosting, setup, or consulting, while retaining MediCore's commercial redistribution guidelines.
3. **Source Code Rights:** Licensees receive 100% unencrypted PHP/React source code. Modification is permitted for private operational use. Public re-hosting, resale on open marketplaces, or sub-licensing to third parties is strictly prohibited.
4. **No Forced Telemetry or Remote Kill Switches:** In alignment with healthcare privacy standards, MediCore operates 100% offline without mandatory outbound licensing phone-home checks, ensuring zero downtime risk.
