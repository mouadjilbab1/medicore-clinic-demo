## 2. Key Commercial Distinctions

### What the Buyer CAN Do:
1. Install and execute the software permanently on their own servers or hosting accounts.
2. Customize, modify, and extend any PHP, React, TypeScript, or database file.
3. White-label the application with their clinic's name, logo, currency, and branding.
4. Manage multiple physical branches or clinical satellite offices under their single clinic organization.
5. Integrate the software with local laboratory analyzers, PACS servers, and billing payment gateways.
6. Operate the software completely offline on an air-gapped local area network (LAN).

### What the Buyer CANNOT Do:
1. Resell, distribute, lease, or publicly share the source code or modified versions to third parties.
2. Launch a public multi-tenant SaaS product that charges other clinics for hosted access to the software.
3. Claim original copyright or inventorship of the underlying architectural codebase.
4. Hold the software developer liable for medical diagnoses, clinical treatment decisions, or data lost due to lack of backups.
